# worst-signal-wins: the mode by the worst signal

Type: decision
Area: task-router
Proof: `plugins/craftlight/skills/task/SKILL.md:35`

## Gist
The mode (S/M/L) = the maximum across the rows of the signals table: files, unambiguity of criteria, reversibility. The worst signal raises the whole mode.

## Rationale
Classification by observable signals, not by time estimation (unreliable for agents). One heavy signal makes the whole task heavy.
